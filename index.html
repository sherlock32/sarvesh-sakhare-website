About Me

Hello! I’m Sarvesh Sakhare, a graduate student in the Operations Research and Information Engineering Department at Cornell University, specializing in Financial Engineering. Before joining Cornell, I spent three years at J.P. Morgan as part of their Quantitative Research team, following my undergraduate studies in Engineering at IIT Madras. My interests lie in leveraging mathematics, programming and machine learning to solve real-world problems. I have been also involved with research in the field of Bayesian Optimisation during my time at Cornell.
